1. Introduction

The Figgy Restaurant App enables restaurants and shop partners to manage their business profiles, product listings and customer orders. This Privacy Policy explains how Figgy collects, uses and protects the information of restaurant and shop partners using the application.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Business name, address and contact details.
  • Owner and authorised staff details.
  • Business registration, GST and applicable licence details.
  • Bank account and settlement information.
  • Product, menu and pricing details.
  • Customer order and transaction information.
  • Device information and app usage data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Register and manage partner accounts.
  • Verify business information.
  • Manage products, menus and availability.
  • Receive, accept, reject and process customer orders.
  • Coordinate order pickups and deliveries.
  • Manage payments and settlements.
  • Send important notifications and updates.
  • Provide support and improve platform services.

4. Information Sharing

Relevant customer order details may be shared with restaurants and shops to fulfil orders. For orders containing products from multiple partners, each partner will receive only the information related to its portion of the order.

Necessary order and pickup information may also be shared with assigned delivery agents. Business and payment information may be shared with authorised payment providers and service partners.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect business, financial and customer information from unauthorised access or misuse. Information will be retained only as necessary for business operations, order management, settlements and legal requirements.

6. Partner Responsibilities

Restaurant and shop partners must maintain the confidentiality of customer information received through Figgy and use it only for order fulfilment. Login credentials and business information must be kept secure and updated.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information and request deletion where applicable under relevant laws. Privacy-related requests can be submitted through our contact details.

8. Policy Updates

Figgy may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be communicated through the application or website.
