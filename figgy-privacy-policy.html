Figgy Customer App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date:13/11/2026

1. Introduction

Figgy is an online food and grocery delivery platform that allows customers to order food and products from multiple restaurants and shops through a single application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use the Figgy Customer App.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Personal details such as name, mobile number and email address.
  • Location and delivery address.
  • Order details, order history and preferences.
  • Payment transaction details and payment status.
  • Device information and app usage data.
  • Feedback, ratings and customer support communications.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process and manage food and grocery orders.
  • Share relevant order details with restaurants and shops.
  • Coordinate deliveries with assigned delivery agents.
  • Process payments and refunds.
  • Send order updates and notifications.
  • Provide customer support and improve our services.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent fraudulent activities.

4. Information Sharing

We may share necessary information with restaurants, shops, delivery agents, payment gateways and other service providers to complete your orders and provide our services. For multiple-shop orders, each restaurant or shop will receive only the relevant order details required to fulfil its part of the order.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure or misuse. Your information will be retained only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfil legal and regulatory requirements.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable laws. You may also withdraw consent where applicable by contacting us.

7. Third-Party Services

Figgy may use third-party services such as payment gateways, maps, cloud hosting and notification providers. Their services may be subject to their respective privacy policies.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published through our application or website.
