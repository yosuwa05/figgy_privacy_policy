Figgy Delivery Agent App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13/11/2026

1. Introduction

The Figgy Delivery Agent App enables delivery agents to receive assigned orders, manage pickups and deliver food and grocery products to customers. This Privacy Policy explains how Figgy collects, uses and protects delivery agents' personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Personal details such as name, mobile number and email address.
  • Identity verification and driving licence details, where required.
  • Vehicle information.
  • Bank account and payout details.
  • GPS location and delivery-related location information.
  • Assigned order and delivery details.
  • Device information and app usage data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Register and verify delivery agent accounts.
  • Assign and manage delivery orders.
  • Display restaurant/shop pickup and customer delivery details.
  • Track delivery progress and update order status.
  • Calculate and process delivery earnings.
  • Send delivery-related notifications.
  • Provide operational support and improve delivery services.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent fraudulent activities.

4. Location Information

The application may access your device location to assign deliveries, coordinate pickups, navigate to customer addresses and update delivery progress. Location information will be used for delivery-related purposes in accordance with applicable permissions and legal requirements.

5. Information Sharing

Necessary customer and restaurant/shop details may be shared with delivery agents to complete assigned deliveries. Delivery agents must use this information only for delivery purposes and must not disclose or misuse customer information.

Relevant banking and transaction details may be shared with authorised payment providers for processing payouts.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable security measures to protect delivery agent information from unauthorised access, disclosure or misuse. Information will be retained only as necessary for account management, delivery operations, payment processing and legal requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information and request deletion where applicable under relevant laws. You may also raise privacy-related concerns through our contact details.

8. Policy Updates

Figgy may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published through the application or website.
